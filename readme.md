### Hi 👋, I am [RAJA MUHAMMAD ASHER](https://rajamuhammadasher.com/)

I started out with PHP, and it’s what got me into web development. I used it to build everything from small dynamic sites to full web applications, and it gave me a solid foundation in backend work, especially writing clean, efficient server-side code.

Wanting to branch out, I picked up Node.js. Its speed and event-driven, non-blocking model changed how I thought about building apps, and it made real-time features much easier to pull off.

Then I found Laravel, which took my PHP work to another level. It’s become my go-to framework because it lets me build clean, scalable, maintainable apps quickly, and its simplicity and flexibility fit the way I like to work.

On the data side, I began with relational databases. I’ve worked with MySQL and MS SQL, designing schemas, optimizing queries, and making sure the data stays consistent. SQL’s strictness sharpened my attention to detail and taught me to value well-organized data.

As projects grew more varied, I moved into NoSQL. Working with MongoDB and Firebase showed me how useful flexible schemas and easy scaling can be when you’re dealing with large volumes of loosely structured data.

None of this happened in isolation. I’ve been lucky to work with talented teammates and good leaders on projects that matter, and those collaborations have improved my work and taught me a lot about keeping up with a fast-changing field.
